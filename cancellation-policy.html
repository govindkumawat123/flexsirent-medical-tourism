    • Cancellation Policy

    We understand plans change — that’s why we keep cancellations flexible and hassle-free. Your peace of mind matters just as much as your next move.

    100% refund when cancelled 5 days before arrival

    Enjoy peace of mind with our flexible cancellation policy

    Before 5 Days

    A full refund will be processed if a guest cancels a booking 5 days before check-in time. For example: If a guest cancels the booking before 3:00 PM on June 04th it is refundable, and will be refunded to their bank account.

    Check-in

    If a guest cancels their booking less than 5 days before the check-in time. This is non-refundable. For example: If a guest cancels the booking after 3:00 PM on June 04th. This is non-refundable.

    Checkout

    The accommodation fees for the night's not spent will not be returned if a guest checks in and decides to leave early. For example: If a guest cancels the booking after 3:00 PM on June 09th, This is non-refundable.

    Important Information

    • Cleaning Fees

      Cleaning fees are always refunded if the guest did not check in.

    • Service Fee

      The Flexsirent service fee is refundable if the guest cancels before the trip starts.

    • Booking Overlap

      If a guest books a reservation that overlaps with any part of an existing reservation, we won't refund the Flexsirent service fee if they decide to cancel.

    • Dispute Resolution

      If there is a complaint from either party, notice must be given to Flexsirent within 24 hours of check-in. Flexsirent will mediate when necessary, and has the final say in all disputes.

    Important Notes
    • A reservation is officially canceled when the guest clicks the cancellation button on the cancellation confirmation page.
    • Cancellation policies may be superseded by the Guest Refund Policy, extenuating circumstances, or cancellations by Flexsirent for any other reason permitted under the Terms of Service.
    • Applicable taxes will be retained and remitted.
