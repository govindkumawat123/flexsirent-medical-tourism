    View Details

    Appointments cancelled 30 or more days before the scheduled treatment date are eligible for a 100% refund (excluding processing fees). Cancellations made 15–29 days prior receive a 50% refund, while cancellations within 14 days are non-refundable. Appointments may be rescheduled subject to doctor availability. If the clinic cancels or reschedules your appointment, you may choose a new appointment date or request a full refund.
